The alphabet and pronunciation

The Greek alphabet has 24 letters. Once you know them, Greek is read almost exactly as it is written. The accent mark (τόνος) shows which syllable is stressed.

Letter Name Sound
Α α άλφα (alfa) a as in father
Β β βήτα (vita) v as in van
Γ γ γάμμα (gama) a soft g; y as in yes before e and i
Δ δ δέλτα (delta) th as in this
Ε ε έψιλον (epsilon) e as in pet
Ζ ζ ζήτα (zita) z as in zoo
Η η ήτα (ita) i as in machine
Θ θ θήτα (thita) th as in think
Ι ι γιώτα (yota) i as in machine
Κ κ κάππα (kapa) k as in kite
Λ λ λάμδα (lamda) l as in lamp
Μ μ μι (mi) m as in mother
Ν ν νι (ni) n as in net
Ξ ξ ξι (ksi) x as in taxi
Ο ο όμικρον (omikron) o as in hot
Π π πι (pi) p as in spin
Ρ ρ ρο (ro) a rolled r
Σ σ ς σίγμα (sigma) s as in sun; ς is used at the end of a word
Τ τ ταυ (taf) t as in stop
Υ υ ύψιλον (ipsilon) i as in machine
Φ φ φι (fi) f as in fun
Χ χ χι (hi) ch as in Scottish loch
Ψ ψ ψι (psi) ps as in lapse
Ω ω ωμέγα (omega) o as in hot

Letter combinations

Some pairs of letters are read as one sound.

Letters Sound
ου u as in food
αι e as in pet
ει, οι, υι i as in machine
αυ, ευ av, ev (af, ef before voiceless consonants)
μπ b at the start of a word, mb inside
ντ d at the start of a word, nd inside
γκ g at the start of a word, ng inside
τσ, τζ ts, dz
